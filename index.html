ABOUT WAKO

WORLD ASSOCIATION OF
KICKBOXING ORGANIZATIONS

WAKO is a leading international organization dedicated to the promotion and development of kickboxing worldwide.

It supports athletes, coaches, referees, and national federations by organizing international competitions, setting rules and standards, and encouraging fair play, discipline, and sportsmanship, making kickboxing accessible to all skill levels while promoting fitness, respect, and excellence.
